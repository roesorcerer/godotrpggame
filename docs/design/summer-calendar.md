# Summer calendar

**Pine Grove runs Monday, June 12 to Sunday, September 3, 2006: 84 days in 12 weeks, building to one crisis night in week 10 and the buyout meeting on day 84.**

*This tab is the **full 12-week vision**. The first release (v1.0) is a 4-week summer, June 12 – July 9, with the crisis night on the Fourth of July; see section 11.5 of the main tab. Week 1 below is the v1.0 tutorial and the demo, unchanged.*

## Pacing rules

| Rule | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| **Drinking nights** | **Random** (5.15): a rising hazard with binges, false calm and promises. Each phase has a target average of about 0, 1, 2 and 3 or more nights a week, but the player never knows which nights. Crisis night is the one fixed night. |
| **Therapy** | Every Friday afternoon in Cobble Creek. Mom drives. After a Thursday drinking night she may not (rolled). With Disclosure 30 or more, Benny can sometimes drive you. |
| **Inner voices** | Active from day 1 (5.3). |
| **Ghosts** | Player-driven: a voice at level 3 draws its ghost. Typical pacing: the first ghost in week 2, the second by week 4, the third by week 6, the fourth only if every voice is grown. |
| **Ghost slots** | Internalize up to 3. |
| **Scripted beats** | One family beat per week, one Friday therapy, and one or two Pine Grove or Cobble Creek events. Every other hour is free. |
| **Money** | The jar needs about $60 every Saturday, and the teen is expected to cover more each phase. Power bill: a shutoff notice in week 5 and the shutoff in week 8 unless it's paid. |
| **Fire season** | Smoke and red-flag warnings from week 8, peaking in weeks 9–10. |
| **Alateen** | Tuesday evenings in Cobble Creek, from week 3 (Eli suggests it). |
| **Bingo** | Every summer evening at the Pine Grove lodge (5.13.3). Going means skipping dinner at home. |
| **Ranch and crops** | The lean-to and workbench in week 1; the car graveyard from week 2; blackberries and jam from week 4 (July–August). |

## Branch points (proposed)

### Bruce's arc

- **Who he is:** contemptuous and proud. His inheritance is nearly gone, and car repairs and guitar tips barely bring anything in. He won't let the teen help with anything ("too skilled to babysit"), and money is the fight underneath every fight with Mom.
- **Branch point 1 (week 6):** he catches you seeing the bank letter that shows the inheritance is gone. Keep quiet, tell Mom, or hold it over him. Telling or holding it over him pushes him toward **hostile**.
- **Branch point 2 (week 8):** with the power cut off, Bruce blames you and searches your room for money. What he finds depends on how you've been hiding it (5.10.2).
- **The crack (any time from week 5, once):** late at night, Sight 3 or more and quiet feet. Bruce is in the barn playing his late father's guitar. He lets you listen to one song, then shuts the door, and never mentions it. Nothing changes mechanically.
- **Result:** **cold** Bruce ignores and dismisses you. **Hostile** Bruce rages, takes money from the jar, and is the one driving on crisis night.

### The witness thread

Disclosure (5.16) rises with honest therapy shares, trusted townies and Alateen.

- **30 or more:** more time away from home: sleepovers at Lilith's, lifts from Benny, and staying late at Pine Grove. Mom starts needling about "those people."
- **60 or more:** Eli must report. A social worker's home visit comes in week 9, or right after the crisis. Home gets worse whatever happens. What you say in the visit decides whether a case opens, is dismissed, or is covered up.

### Crisis night (Sat, Aug 19, day 69)

On a red-flag night after a drinking binge, Mom or Bruce grabs the keys to drive Oscar down the grade. How it goes depends on what the teen has become:

| Outcome | Requires (roughly) | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stop it** | Oscar's trust high, Spirit not empty, and at least one of Tracy's nerve or Ray's strength | You get the keys, keep Oscar home, and call for help (June's lesson: ask). The pattern breaks. |
| **Go with them** | Caretaker voices (Ray's Grit or June's Heart), lower trust | You get in the car to protect Oscar. The car goes off the road on the grade; everyone survives, shaken. No one is told. |
| **Not there** | High Reliance, sneaked out, or isolating | You find out after. Oscar is hurt (not killed) and in the hospital. The family fractures. |

Oscar never dies. The game is about breaking a pattern, not punishing the player with a child's death. All three outcomes feed the epilogue slides (7.5).
